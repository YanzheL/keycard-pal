Ethereum reviews show what a contract call or EIP-712 message does when the ERC-7730 clear-signing registry describes it, and whether an auditor reviewed that description. Settings can import a newer registry, and the full build can download one. A transaction is reviewed and signed for the chain it names itself. The scanner reads again after leaving a review, the app no longer crashes when the font size or language changes, and screen readers skip a blurred PUK or recovery phrase.
